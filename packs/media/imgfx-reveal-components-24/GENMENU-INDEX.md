# GENMENU-INDEX — Image-Overlay & Reveal Component Family (24)

A self-contained family of 24 editorial HTML components built with Tailwind CSS CDN, vanilla inline JavaScript, photographic CSS/SVG gradients with turbulence grain filters, light/dark theme support, keyboard focusability, and `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance.

## A. Hover-Reveal Image Cards (8)

- **`imgfx-slide-reveal-01.html`** — Image lifts and scales back on hover or focus to uncover an architectural caption and editorial link underneath.
- **`imgfx-diagonal-wipe-02.html`** — Full-bleed photographic tint shears away diagonally on hover to expose high-contrast studio specifications underneath.
- **`imgfx-mask-expand-03.html`** — Shaped architectural aperture mask expands smoothly across the frame on hover while the underlying CTA panel locks into view.
- **`imgfx-duotone-rise-04.html`** — Editorial grid tile grades into warm monochrome duotone as a crisp project year and archive link panel rises from the base.
- **`imgfx-split-doors-05.html`** — Twin synchronized image halves part horizontally on hover or focus to reveal centered exhibition typography between the shutters.
- **`imgfx-blur-focus-06.html`** — Depth-of-field blur and measured scrim darken the plate on hover while sharp typographic metadata and action controls fade in.
- **`imgfx-bottom-sheet-07.html`** — Hairline underline sweeps left-to-right to anchor an interior bottom sheet carrying material disciplines and a directional arrow.
- **`imgfx-kenburns-glass-08.html`** — Continuous slow optical drift pauses cleanly on hover or focus while a frosted architectural glass dossier glides into place.

## B. Scroll-Revealed Overlays (5)

- **`imgfx-pinned-split-09.html`** — Sticky photographic viewport cross-fades across four editorial chapters as the narrative column scrolls beside it.
- **`imgfx-scroll-overlap-10.html`** — Full-bleed architectural plate subtly scales down as an editorial essay card glides upward over the lower horizon.
- **`imgfx-clip-wipe-11.html`** — Successive full-viewport photographic plates wipe smoothly over the previous frame via scroll-linked CSS inset() clipping.
- **`imgfx-sequence-scrub-12.html`** — Eight continuous daylight-to-twilight lighting frames scrub seamlessly with scroll progress like a tactile cinema reel.
- **`imgfx-curtain-part-13.html`** — Twin photographic curtain panels part left and right upon entering the viewport to unveil the studio manifesto beneath.

## C. Cursor-Follow + List Reveals (4)

- **`imgfx-cursor-float-14.html`** — Agency index rows drive a spring-damped floating photographic plate that follows the pointer and swaps per hovered project.
- **`imgfx-word-scale-15.html`** — Editorial roster of fashion and spatial terms spawns an oversized lookbook preview scaling outward from the cursor origin.
- **`imgfx-nav-backdrop-16.html`** — Minimalist studio navigation cross-dissolves distinct full-bleed atmospheric backdrops behind the typography on hover or focus.
- **`imgfx-cursor-spotlight-17.html`** — Masked low-key monochrome plate illuminates in full warm color inside a smooth circular radial aperture tracking the cursor.

## D. Image Transition States (4)

- **`imgfx-before-after-18.html`** — Precision comparison frame with pointer-drag and arrow-key divider comparing raw concrete formwork against finished timber.
- **`imgfx-cross-dissolve-19.html`** — Dual-state architectural plates cross-dissolve smoothly between sunlit limestone and nocturnal tungsten illumination on hover.
- **`imgfx-flip-expand-20.html`** — Contact-sheet thumbnails expand smoothly from their exact grid bounds into a full-bleed captioned lightbox using FLIP geometry.
- **`imgfx-frame-swap-21.html`** — Editorial specimen tile transitions cleanly out of the photograph and into a typeset curator note within the identical frame.

## E. Text + Media Sizzle (3)

- **`imgfx-text-clip-22.html`** — Monumental display headline masks a layered photographic gradient field inside its letterforms that shifts and breathes on hover.
- **`imgfx-velocity-marquee-23.html`** — Continuous studio filmstrip marquee accelerates and tilts subtly with scroll velocity while pausing smoothly on pointer hover.
- **`imgfx-scramble-hero-24.html`** — Full-bleed architectural hero runs a crisp typographic decode sequence on load and re-triggers cleanly on hover or keyboard focus.
